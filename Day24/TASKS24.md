# Day 24 – Linear Systems & Decomposition (AI/ML 100 Days Challenge)

## 🎯 Goal of the Day

Understand how **systems of linear equations** are solved efficiently and how **matrix decomposition** techniques (LU, SVD) power many real-world ML applications.

---

## ⏰ Time Breakdown (Total ~5 Hours)

### 📘 1. Lecture – Linear Systems & Decomposition (1.5h)

**Topics to cover:**

* Linear systems and equations

  * Understanding **Ax = b**
  * When solutions exist (unique, infinite, none)
* Solving linear systems:

  * Gaussian elimination (high-level idea)
* LU Decomposition:

  * What is LU decomposition?
  * Why break A into L and U?
  * How it helps in faster solving
* Singular Value Decomposition (SVD):

  * What is SVD?
  * Meaning of U, Σ, Vᵀ
  * Why SVD is important in ML

📌 *Suggested resources*: 3Blue1Brown, StatQuest, Khan Academy
